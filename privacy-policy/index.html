This policy covers the website at cornerstonediscovery.com, operated by Cornerstone Discovery, {{ site.company.address }}, {{ site.company.city_state_zip }}. It does not cover information we receive in the course of client engagements, which is governed by our engagement agreements and applicable protective orders.

Information you give us

When you use our contact form we collect the name, company, email address, phone number, and message you enter, plus the address of the page you sent it from. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and to follow up about our services. The form is delivered to our staff by email through our email delivery provider (Mailgun); we do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or rent your information.

Spam protection

The contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to tell people from automated spam. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and browser data, and sends it to Google for analysis. Its use is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are read and whether the contact form or our phone and email links are used. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, browser and device type, and pages visited. We do not use it to identify individual visitors. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Meta (Facebook) Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel to measure visits from our Facebook page and any Facebook or Instagram advertising we run. It tells Meta which pages were viewed and whether the contact form or our phone and email links were used; it does not send what you type into the form. Meta may link this to your Facebook or Instagram account under its own Privacy Policy. You can control ad personalization in your Meta ad preferences or by blocking third-party cookies in your browser.

{%- endif %}

What we don't do

This site does not use call-tracking or phone-number-swapping services{% unless site.analytics.meta_pixel_id %}, advertising pixels from social networks,{% endunless %} or third-party advertising networks, and we do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Inquiries are kept in our business email for as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of our communications. The site itself stores nothing: it is a static website, and the form is processed in transit and not stored by the site.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email {{ site.company.email }} or call {{ site.company.phone }}.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
