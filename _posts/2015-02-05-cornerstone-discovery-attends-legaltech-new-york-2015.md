### Team Launches New Company Brand and Junto Web Application at New York Event.

![](/uploads/2021/02/legaltech-2015-1.jpg)

(From Right to Left) Jason Silva, Louis Cinquanto, Joe Walsh and Grady Walters at LegalTech New York

### A NEW YORK MINUTE

“A year in the making and with all the excitement and enthusiasm it felt like it went by in a New York minute” said Louis Cinquanto. In addition, “I’m so proud of the team and their efforts to the Company ready for this event.”

On February 3rd the team headed to “the Big Apple” as a vital player in this year’s LegalTech event. “LegalTech New York is by far the largest and most important legal technology event of the year. And we want to be center stage when it comes to law firms, corporations and government agencies seeking partnerships and support services to improve their litigation efforts.” said Louis Cinquanto. In addition, “The event played a pivotal role in launching the new brand (Cornerstone Discovery), Junto web application and generating awareness for our suite of service offerings.”
