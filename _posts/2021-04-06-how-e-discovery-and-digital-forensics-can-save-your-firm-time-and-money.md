[e-Discovery](/e-discovery/) refers to evidentiary material stored in electronic format for use in litigation. e-Discovery material features metadata that doesn't exist in paper documents that can be instrumental, including the date and time files were created or altered, copyright information, and other pertinent data.

## **How Does e-Discovery Help in a Complicated Litigation Case?**

Finding proof in complex litigation can be a challenge. In our current digital world, data grows rapidly. This proliferation drives up case expenses, primarily if you rely on traditional manual methods to attain essential information. Reputable e-Discovery vendors can sort, process, and authenticate large pieces of data in a fast and organized manner.

e-Discovery helps attorneys find the document that helps craft a story and drive a narrative that proves their case beyond a reasonable doubt while keeping costs minimal. Ideally, technology experts can manage e-Discovery to reduce the stress typically associated with high-stakes complex litigation. e-Discovery also enables you to utilize the best practice approach and develop a solid proof trial strategy for all future arguments, motions, and depositions.

## **When Should You Seek the Help of a Digital Forensic Expert?**

![](/uploads/2021/04/Cornerstone-Discovery_2021_Infographic_revised.png)

If you are involved in litigation matters that require significant amounts of digital evidence, it is advisable to work with experienced [digital forensic examiners](/digital-forensics/). Digital forensics demands specialized expertise to recover and analyze evidence while ensuring the fairness, discretion, and sensitivity of confidential data.

Here are a few extra reasons to work with a trusted digital forensic team:

### **Dealing with a sensitive matter**

You may need to engage an impartial digital forensic team when you have a sensitive matter that requires neutrality. Such situations involve mining protected private data crucial to the case. An unbiased digital team keeps personal information confidential while preserving and procuring relevant findings.

### **Investigations that need certainty**

If you want an assurance that the digital evidence you are relying on was collected in a defensible manner and analyzed correctly, it is imperative to employ a digital forensics team that prepares the data in a way that can stand up to careful observation.
