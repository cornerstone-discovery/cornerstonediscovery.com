**Visit Cornerstone Discovery at the 2017 Joint Annual Conference.**

Each year, Criminal Defense Attorneys from every corner of Pennsylvania gather together for an elite networking and comprehensive educational event. Cornerstone Discovery is pleased to attend the 2017 Joint Annual Conference of the Pennsylvania Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers (PACDL), as the only full service litigation support firm in attendance.

![](/uploads/2021/01/blind-justice.webp)

PACDL is a statewide nonprofit association representing more than 1,000 private and public defense lawyers throughout the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. The organization serves to represent criminal defense practitioners in matters of government relations, provide meaningful professional development opportunities and advance leadership among criminal defense lawyers.

The two-day conference will be held at the Hilton in Harrisburg, PA from April 27-28, 2017. Cornerstone Discovery invites you to stop by their exhibitor booth for information about their leading edge services in Digital Forensics, eDiscovery, Litigation Support and Trial Support. [Click here](/uploads/2021/02/pacdl-schedule.pdf) to view the conference schedule.

Visit [pacdl.org](https://www.pacdl.org/) for more information on their Annual Membership Meeting set for Friday, April 28, 2017.
