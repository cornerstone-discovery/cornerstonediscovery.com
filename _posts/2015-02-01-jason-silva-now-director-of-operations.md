### COMPANY VISION AND DEDICATION MAKES HIM THE RIGHT CHOICE

Certified Digital Forensic Examiner, Jason Silva, joined our Company in 2010. Jason soon became a devoted apprentice to Louis Cinquanto, establishing a rigorous work ethic and desire to advance the company via technical innovation.
