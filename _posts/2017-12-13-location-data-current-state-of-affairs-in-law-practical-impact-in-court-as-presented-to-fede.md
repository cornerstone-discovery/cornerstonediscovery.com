Certified Digital Forensic Examiner, ACE and Director, Jason Silva, recently joined Abraham J. Rein, Esq. of [Post & Schell](https://www.postschell.com/), P.C. and Angie Halim, Esq. of Halim Drossner, P.C. to present to the [Federal Criminal Law Committee](https://www.fbacrimphila.org/) in Philadelphia. The team was invited to share their expertise in the areas of Digital Forensics, Information Privacy/Security and Criminal Defense Law.

The presentation took place at the offices of [Post & Schell](https://www.postschell.com/) where various members of the Federal Criminal Law Committee gathered together.

![](/uploads/2021/01/gavel-data.webp)

The team captivated guests with their passion and insights in their respective areas of proficiency. Jason Silva shared his experience in the practical application of location data including type, storage and interpretation of data, as well as the accuracy of data and how investigators are using it. Abraham J. Rein, Esq. and Angie Halim, Esq. discussed the current state of affairs in law relative to stored communication and the impact in court.
