Winning a case involves a number of moving parts, including thorough preparation, a team working seamlessly together, and an infallible presentation. It’s impossible to achieve a favorable outcome alone, which is why trial attorneys and their assistants rely on [trial support services](/trial-support/).

Trial support specialists can collect and organize essential documents and information, effectively eliminating many of the challenges of presenting evidence at court that lawyers face. These specialists can remove some of the tasks on your plate as a trial attorney and allow you to focus on retaining clients and winning claims. If you’re not sure hiring a trial support specialist is right for you, review these benefits of utilizing their professional trial presentation services during your case:

## **Make a Strong First Impression**

During a trial, you’re not just presenting a case; you’re telling a story. This means you need to communicate your story in a way that influences the jury to side with your client. Trial presentations prepared by support specialists using advanced technology can allow you to share your client’s claim in the most effective way possible, leaving a lasting impression on the jury.

## **Keep Documents and Evidence Organized**

With so many tasks on your plate, it can be challenging to find time to organize all the files, documents, and digital evidence related to your client’s case. Fortunately, you can hand this time-consuming task off to trial support specialists, and they’ll organize jury handbooks, presentations, and witness kits for you to use during your upcoming trial.

Having digital copies of all your evidence means you don’t have to worry about lugging around thick stacks of printouts. Additionally, you can pull up exhibits or transcripts at a moment’s notice. Everything you need to make a strong case is literally at your fingertips.

## **Ensure Flexibility With On-Demand Edits**

Trial presentations require a certain amount of flexibility. Parts of exhibits or depositions can be redacted before being presented to the jury. When this happens, edits need to be made at the last minute, which can be tricky with digital evidence. Thankfully, you don’t have to worry about making last-minute edits when you have a skilled technician on your team. They will have access to high-quality photo and video editing software that will allow them to perform any modifications required at a moment’s notice. You can rest assured that you’ll have the necessary flexibility without having to learn new software.

## **Stay Ahead of the Competition**

As technology advances, trial attorneys are expected to present information quickly and concisely. By hiring a trial support technician, you can ensure your presentation meets (and exceeds) expectations. Jurors and judges will appreciate your organized presentation, and it may just help you out-perform your opponent.

## **Allow You to Focus on What You Do Best**

A trial technician can make your job life easier by taking care of the technical aspects and logistics of your presentation. They will ensure you can provide an incredible presentation — no matter the state of the courtroom and whether it is equipped with projectors, screens, DVD players, or nothing at all. With a trial specialist on your team, you will know everything you need will be in order and easily accessible when the time is right. Leading up to and during the trial, you can focus on what you do best: building and defending a strong case for your client.
