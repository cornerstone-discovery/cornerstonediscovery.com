### Students Learn First Hand About Today’s Data-Driven World And What We Do.

![](/uploads/2021/02/NFCS-Career-Day-2015.jpg)

[NFCS](https://www.nfcsonline.org) 10th Grade Students in the Conference Room Learning about [Junto](https://www.junto.net/) — online review platform

On Wednesday, November 11th Cornerstone Discovery hosted 10th grade students from the New Foundations Charter School (NFCS) for the 5th Annual NFCS Career Shadow Day.

As part one of a two-day event, the students spent the afternoon learning first hand from our industry experts about [Cornerstone Discovery](/) and our areas of specialization including: Digital Forensics, E-Discovery, Litigation/Trial Support, and Software Solutions. In addition, the students spoke directly with employees and took a tour of the office, data, and network operations center (NOC).

For this event, NFCS sent 212 of their 10th grade students to approximately 88 businesses and organizations across the city of Philadelphia to shadow professionals in a wide range of select career fields — making it the largest NFCS Shadow Day to date.

Cornerstone Discovery is proud to be an NFCS Community Sponsor.
