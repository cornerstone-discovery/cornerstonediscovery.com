**8 years, 11 prisoners released, 227 years of injustice…**

![](/uploads/2021/01/penn-innocence-1.webp)

Cornerstone Discovery is proud to support the [Pennsylvania Innocence Project](https://innocenceprojectpa.org/) as *Freedom Fighters* at their Annual Event on May 11, 2017.

The event will honor “Lifelines,” those on the outside who provide the innocent with the hope and strength to achieve exoneration. The Pennsylvania Innocence Project celebrates eight years of unrelenting effort and growth. The Project has proudly led to the release of eleven wrongly convicted people who had served a total of 277 years in prison.

![](/uploads/2021/01/penn-innoceence-2.webp)

We pay tribute to the founders, David Richman and David Rudovsky, who have demonstrated the grit, vision and persistence to create the Project. We salute the staff and countless volunteers who empower the mission. We commend all lifelines who supply emotional sustenance and take action to collect evidence in order to help assist in exoneration. It is your collaboration and passionate efforts that allow the Pennsylvania Innocence Project to make a difference.

Learn more about three families, the lifelines, who stood resolutely by their loved ones until their recent freedom. It's our honor to support the Pennsylvania Innocence Project and celebrate Ken Granger and his daughter Tanya, Gene Gilyard and his mother Christine, and Donte Rollins and his mother Ava.
