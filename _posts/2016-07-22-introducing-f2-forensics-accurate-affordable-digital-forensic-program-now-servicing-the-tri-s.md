### **Cornerstone Discovery Launches Game-Changing, Turn-Key Digital Forensic Program for Mobile Devices. Not Only Is It Accurate and Affordable – It’s Fast 2.**

Today, July 22, 2016, Cornerstone Discovery officially launches F2 Forensics program to service the legal and investigative community in the tri-state area.

Attorneys, law firms, and investigators within PA, NJ and DE now have a convenient, reliable and local service to quickly obtain Certified Forensic Reports on any mobile device. F2 Forensics is an affordable Digital Forensic program with straight-forward flat fee pricing. What’s more? Turnaround times with F2 are as little as one day.

F2 Forensics helps you avoid claims of spoliation with proper preservation that adheres to today’s strict data preservation standards for discovery.

Our Certified Digital Forensic Experts will preserve the data and extract all recoverable data on any mobile device — Apple iOS, Google Android, BlackBerry and more. This includes but is not limited to:

- Mobile Phones
- iPads
- Tablets
- GPS Devices

With F2 our Certified Digital Forensic Examiners will provide you with the following:

- Expert Preservation of data on the mobile device — avoid spoliation or improper preservation
- Forensic extraction of all recoverable data from the mobile device — includes deleted chats, photos and more
- Chain of custody + full extraction report + F2 Certified Forensic Report

F2 Certified Forensic Reports are the same type of reports used by the Police, FBI and other investigative government agencies. It provides a wealth of knowledge for attorneys and investigators in a simple to understand format.
