### Louis Cinquanto to Present to New York State Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers (NYSACDL).

*[NYSACDL](http://www.nysacdl.org) — Promoting Excellence in Criminal Defense*

EnCE and Managing Director of Cornerstone Discovery, [Louis Cinquanto](http://www.cornerstonediscovery.com/about-us.php#headingThree) will be a guest speaker at the upcoming [NYSACDL](http://www.nysacdl.org) CLE Seminar — Federal Practice 2016 at New York Law School on Friday, February 5, 2016.

**When**

- Friday, February 5, 2016
- 11:00 AM - 12:45 PM

**Where**

- [New York Law School](https://www.google.com/maps/place/185+W+Broadway,+New+York,+NY+10013/@40.7180192,-74.0091623,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m2!3m1!1s0x89c25a1ff1651e0d:0xef340585d0fb4051)
- 185 W Broadway
- New York, NY 10013

**Topic**

- Examining Cell Phone Evidence & Other Technology Discovery Issues

[NYSACDL](http://www.nysacdl.org/) is dedicated to protecting the rights of criminal defendants through a strong, unified, and well-trained criminal defense bar. The NYSACDL’s guiding principle is that vigorous defense is the strongest bulwark against error and injustice in the criminal justice system. In an era when the United States has the highest incarceration rate in the world, The NYSACDL expands on the question most often posed to their members and asks “how can we defend those people most effectively?”
