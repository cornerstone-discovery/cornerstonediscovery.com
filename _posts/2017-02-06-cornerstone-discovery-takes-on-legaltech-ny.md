Cornerstone Discovery’s Senior Developer, Kyle McArdle, and Director of Business Development, Robert Centofanti, traveled to New York last week to take part in the excitement of [Le](https://legalweekshow.com/legaltech/east/)[galTech 2017](https://legalweekshow.com/legaltech/east/). We joined our friends at [Accusoft](https://www.accusoft.com/), leading technology providers in the document imaging and content management industry, to help them show off our innovative e-Discovery collaboration: [PrizmDoc](https://www.accusoft.com/products/prizmdoc/overview/) meets [Junto](https://junto.net/).

Hundreds of industry professionals stopped by our booth to see how Accusoft’s online viewing product, PrizmDoc, empowers our e-Discovery platform, [Junto](https://junto.net/), with exceptional user interface.

As always, our team scoured the technical and legal space for this year’s most innovative new services, products and partnerships, collecting inspiration and ideas to help better serve our Clients with future offerings. Collaboration is at the heart of our practice; we took the time to establish new relationships during the conference and caught up with our current partners too.

Stop by our office for the sea of inspiration we collected. With nearly 10,000 attendees, from participants to speakers and exhibitors alike, we gathered hundreds of pamphlets, ideas and new contacts, to continue to provide our valued Clients with the most innovative solutions and stellar service the industry has to offer.

Stay tuned for news about our newest product launch and see how LegalTech continues to inspire us to push the boundaries in today’s technical legal space.
