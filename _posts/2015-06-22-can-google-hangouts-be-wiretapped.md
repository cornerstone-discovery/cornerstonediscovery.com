### Technically Possible for Google to Wiretap Conversations at the Request of Law Enforcement Agents.

![](/uploads/2021/02/hangouts-2.png)

Google Hangouts

In an article written by Lorenzo Franceschi-Bicchierai from [Motherboard.vice.com](https://motherboard.vice.com/en_us), he reports Google’s popular Hangouts service, which can be used for both text-based as well as audio-video conversations, doesn’t use end-to-end encryption. As a result, this makes it technically possible for Google to wiretap conversations at the request of law enforcement agents.
