![Award badge for the Legal Intelligencer's Best of 2025 Winner](/uploads/2025/09/best-of-2025-general-winner.png)

We are thrilled to announce that Cornerstone Discovery has been recognized as a top service provider by Pennsylvania’s legal community in The Legal Intelligencer’s 16th annual “Best Of” survey!

Every year, The Legal Intelligencer polls its audience of attorneys and legal professionals to identify the best products and service providers in the industry. This survey is a vital resource, helping law firms and their teams choose the partners who can help them achieve the best results and compete effectively in today’s demanding legal market.

This year, we are proud to have placed in five categories and received Hall of Fame recognition, which is reserved for companies that have consistently ranked in the “Best Of” survey for the past three years.

## **Voted the #1 Digital Forensic Company of 2025**

![award for the winner of "Digital Forensic Company" from The Legal Intelligencer](/uploads/2025/09/image.png)

For the third consecutive year, we have been voted the #1 Digital Forensic Company in The Legal Intelligencer’s survey. This comes as no surprise to our team, as [digital forensics](/digital-forensics/) is at the core of what we do. When you partner with us, you can be confident that our certified experts will employ cutting-edge techniques to uncover, analyze, and preserve critical data in a manner that is both defensible and admissible.

## **Ranked #3 in Trial Technology**

![award for a Hall of Fame member in the category "Trial Technology" from The Legal Intelligencer](/uploads/2025/09/image-1.png)

Our second Hall of Fame recognition comes in the Trial Technology category. This award reflects our long-term commitment to providing the legal community with reliable and innovative technology solutions.

From pre-trial strategy to courtroom presentations, our technology is designed to create a smooth and impactful experience. No matter if you’re managing a criminal or civil litigation case, we will equip your legal team with the state-of-the-art tools you need to present your case with confidence.

## **Ranked #3 in Hot Seat Trial Technicians**

A compelling courtroom presentation can make all the difference, which is why legal teams consistently rely on our [trial support experts](/trial-support/). Being ranked among the top three for Hot Seat Trial Technicians underscores our team’s ability to perform under pressure. We will help you tell a persuasive story by managing the technology in the courtroom. In this way, you can focus on advocating for your client in the best way possible without having to worry about technology snafus.

## **Ranked #3 in Demonstrative Evidence Provider**

Complex information is often best understood visually. As a leading demonstrative evidence provider, we consistently transform complicated data into clear and persuasive visual aids. These demonstratives will enhance juror comprehension and retention so that you can make a stronger argument.

## **Ranked #3 in End-to-End eDiscovery**

Rounding out our wins is a third-place finish and a Hall of Fame recognition for our end-to-end eDiscovery services. Our professionals can manage the entire eDiscovery lifecycle, from preservation and collection to review and production, no matter the specifics of your litigation case. We provide a streamlined, efficient, and defensible process that saves you time, reduces costs, and mitigates risk.

## **Thank You for Your Support!**

We are incredibly grateful to our clients and the readers of The Legal Intelligencer for their continued trust and support. These awards inspire us to continue raising the bar in digital forensics, eDiscovery, and trial technology.
