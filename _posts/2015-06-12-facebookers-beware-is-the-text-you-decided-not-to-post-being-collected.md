### Users May Want to be More Cautious About What They Enter Into Status Updates or Comments Prior to Posting.

![](/uploads/2021/02/kyle-fb2.png)

Facebook

In a recent article posted by Ben Rossi on Information-Age, a technology consultant based in Ireland claims Facebook collects all content that is typed into its website, even if it’s not posted. After being approached by [Information Age,](https://www.information-age.com/) Facebook has denied the allegation. However, the technology consultant claims this is not the case after he inspected Facebook’s network traffic through a developer tool and screencasting software. Users may want to be more cautious about what they enter into status updates or comments prior to posting.
