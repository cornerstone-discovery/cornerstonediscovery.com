**Louis Cinquanto was invited to be a guest speaker at Pennsylvania Bar Institute’s (PBI) 34th Annual Criminal Law Symposium.**

PBI strives to bring excellence to CLE. Its mission is to provide Pennsylvania lawyers with information that is timely, practical, cost-effective, and of the highest possible quality. Over 300 attorneys were present for PBI’s two-day event in Harrisburg, PA (June 8-9, 2017).

Among the twenty-plus guest speakers, Cornerstone Discovery’s Louis Cinquanto (EnCE, ACE) presented and spoke with attendees about the ever-increasing sources of Electronically Stored Information (ESI) including the Internet of Things (IoT) and Body Cams. Specifically, his topic “Alexa, Body Cams & Other Technology” was center stage for roughly one hour on Friday.
