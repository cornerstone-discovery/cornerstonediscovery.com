### Former Internet Crime Detective/Computer Forensics Examiner Now On Board.

*Joe Walsh, EnCE - Director, Digital Forensics*

## Not Your Average Joe

In January, 2014 the Company announced its newest team member to join our Digital Forensics team. Joe Walsh is now Director, Digital Forensics at Cornerstone Discovery. His extensive experience and exclusive training in computer and cellular forensic technology makes him an influential and vital member of our team.

Holding a Bachelor of Science in Information Systems and a Master of Arts in Criminal Justice, with a concentration in Digital Forensics, his dedication to law enforcement and passion for technology has naturally positioned him into his current role. Joe has been serving his community as a Police Officer since 2008. Before joining our team, Joe spent several years working as an Internet Crime Detective/Computer Forensics Examiner for the Delaware County Criminal Investigation Division. Joe also served as a member of the Federal Bureau of Investigation Child Exploitation Task Force. Joe’s abundant level of experience and advanced technical proficiency make him a highly regarded expert, previously testifying in State court as an Expert in Computer Forensics.
