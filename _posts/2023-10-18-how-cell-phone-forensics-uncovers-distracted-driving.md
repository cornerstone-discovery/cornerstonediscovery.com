## **The Power of Cell Phone Forensics in Proving Distracted Driving**

The United States has witnessed a surge in the number of accidents attributed to distracted driving, predominantly involving the usage of cell phones while operating a vehicle. Cell phone forensics provides a unique opportunity to establish a direct link between cell phone usage and distracted driving incidents. By employing advanced [phone data extraction methods](/what-are-the-different-data-extraction-methods-for-mobile-devices/), forensic examiners can reconstruct a driver's actions leading up to a collision. This might include reviewing:

- **Call Logs:** Call logs provide a detailed record of incoming and outgoing phone calls, offering insights into conversations that may have taken place while the driver was on the road. This information can help establish whether the driver was actively engaged in a conversation at the time of the accident.
- **Text Messages:** Extracting the content of sent and received messages can reveal whether the driver was composing or reading texts while driving. Such evidence is crucial in cases where texting is prohibited by law.
- **App Usage Data:** App usage data presents a comprehensive overview of the applications the driver interacted with. This can include social media platforms, navigation apps, and entertainment apps. Identifying active app usage during the time of the accident can help reconstruct the driver's level of engagement with the phone.
- **Browsing History:** Browsing history can disclose whether the driver was browsing the internet while driving, potentially indicating further distractions.

With Cornerstone Discovery, our digital forensic experts can also perform advanced extractions to help unlock detailed information from device logs. This includes:

- **Device Locks:** Information about device locks, such as PINs or passwords, can indicate whether the driver was attempting to unlock the phone while driving, suggesting a possible distraction.
- **Device Orientation:** Data about the device's orientation can reveal whether the phone was held in a way that suggests active engagement by the driver at the time of the accident.
- **Device Events:** Records of device events, such as screen taps or button presses, can provide insights into the driver's interactions with the phone while driving.
- **Power Logs:** Power logs track when the device is powered on or off. Analyzing these logs can help establish whether the driver turned the phone on or off during their journey.

One of the most compelling aspects of cell phone forensics in distracted driving cases is its ability to corroborate eyewitness testimonies. In legal proceedings, eyewitness accounts often vary and may lack concrete evidence. However, digital forensics can bridge this gap by presenting concrete data that can substantiate claims made by those who witnessed the accident. This is particularly crucial in cases where a distracted driver denies phone usage as a contributing factor.
