### Jason Finishes in Palmerton, PA at The 2015 Spartan Super.

![](/uploads/2021/02/jay.jpg)

Jason Silva Sporting His 2015 Spartan Finisher Metal In His Favorite Junto T-Shirt

Congratulations to [Jason Silva](/about-us/)! The Cornerstone Discovery, Director of Operations recently completed the 8+ mile Spartan Super Race at Blue Mountain in Palmerton, PA while sporting his favorite [Junto t-shirt.](https://www.junto.net/) Spartan Races feature a series of obstacles over varying distances and difficulties.

It should come to no surprise that our Director of Operations was up for this challenge. Jason maintains the training, physical tools and mindset to overcome these obstacles. He took on the same approach when developing [Junto](https://www.junto.net/) with his team of programmers and designers. [Junto](https://www.junto.net/) helps Law, Business and Government professionals tackle the obstacles of Discovery. With [Junto](https://www.junto.net/) E-Discovery Just Got Easier.
