Digital Forensics and Litigation Support for Criminal Defense

Digital evidence is in nearly every criminal case now: phone extractions, cell site records, surveillance video, social media and terabytes of discovery. Since 2005, Cornerstone Discovery has given criminal defense attorneys, public defenders and court-appointed counsel the same caliber of technical expertise the prosecution brings to court.

We work with defense teams nationwide, in state and federal courts, from the first review of discovery through testimony and trial presentation.

The government has its examiners. You should have yours.

DISCUSS YOUR CASE

Independent Review of the Government’s Digital Evidence

Forensic reports produced in discovery show what the examiner chose to look for. We re-examine the extractions and images behind them, check the tools and methods used, and look for what was left out: messages that support the defense, location data that contradicts the timeline, or artifacts that point to another user of the device.

Cell Site and Location Evidence

When the case turns on where a phone was, our cell site analysis team, led by a former wireless carrier testifier, maps the call detail records, tests the prosecution’s conclusions, and explains the limits of tower-based location to the jury.

Forensic Examinations for the Defense

With proper authorization, we preserve and examine the phones, computers and online accounts that matter to your client’s case, including damaged or locked devices, and recover the deleted messages, photos and app data that can corroborate a defense.

Video and Surveillance Footage

We collect footage from DVR systems in a forensically sound way, verify timestamps and metadata, and prepare clear clips and stills for court.

Discovery Management for Large Cases

Federal and multi-defendant cases can produce more discovery than a defense team can open, let alone read. Our review platform, Junto, was built to manage large federal criminal discovery: documents, audio and video, searchable in one place, with access for the whole defense team.

Trial Presentation

Our trial technicians run the courtroom technology, so exhibits, video and recordings appear the moment you need them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you review the forensic report the prosecution produced?

Yes. We review the report and, where it is available, the underlying extraction or image, and tell you where the findings hold up and where they go further than the data supports.

Can you examine my client’s phone?

Yes, with proper authorization. We preserve the device first with documented chain of custody, then examine it. If the government holds the device or its extraction, we can help you request access in discovery.

Do you testify in federal court?

Yes. Our examiners have been qualified as expert witnesses in local, state and federal courts.

Do you work outside Pennsylvania?

Yes. We work with defense teams nationwide. Evidence and discovery can be delivered to us securely, and our experts travel to testify.
