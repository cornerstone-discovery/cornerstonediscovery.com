What Are Paper Discovery Services?

Despite the rapid adoption of digital evidence, many legal matters still involve extensive collections of physical documents that must be carefully organized, reviewed, and produced. Contracts, medical records, financial statements, personnel files, handwritten notes, correspondence, archived business records, and other paper files often play a critical role in litigation.

Without an organized approach, these materials can slow litigation, lead to missing or duplicated documents, complicate attorney review, and increase the likelihood of production errors. That's why law firms, corporations, government agencies, and organizations continue to rely on experienced litigation support professionals to streamline paper discovery from beginning to end.

Our paper discovery services involve the collection, organization, processing, digitization, and production of physical documents used during litigation, investigations, regulatory matters, and other legal proceedings. We handle every page carefully to preserve document integrity, maintain chain of custody, and ensure accurate organization throughout the discovery process.

Our goal is to simplify complex paper discovery projects while helping attorneys focus on building stronger cases rather than managing overwhelming volumes of documents.

Our Comprehensive Paper Discovery Process

Every litigation matter presents unique challenges, which is why Cornerstone Discovery follows a carefully structured process that emphasizes accuracy, consistency, and efficiency throughout every stage of paper discovery.

Secure Document Collection

Our team carefully inventories document collections while maintaining proper handling procedures to protect sensitive information throughout transportation and processing. Whether documents originate from a law office, corporate archive, healthcare provider, or government agency, we ensure they remain organized from the very beginning.

Document Indexing

Before processing begins, documents are organized into logical categories that simplify review and retrieval. Proper indexing creates a clear structure that helps attorneys quickly locate specific records while preserving relationships between documents, folders, and files.

Effective indexing also reduces unnecessary duplication and improves consistency across large discovery projects involving thousands or millions of pages.

Document Scanning

High-quality legal document scanning forms the foundation of an efficient paper discovery workflow. Using professional legal imaging equipment, our team captures clear, accurate images while preserving every page's integrity.

OCR Conversion for Searchable Documents

Once documents have been scanned, Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology converts image files into searchable text. This important step transforms static paper documents into searchable digital records that attorneys can quickly review using keywords, names, dates, or phrases. This paper-to-digital eDiscovery step significantly reduces the time required to locate relevant information while improving overall review efficiency.

Document Coding

Proper document coding provides additional organization by assigning metadata and other identifying information to individual records. Coding may include document dates, authors, custodians, Bates numbers, privilege indicators, issue tags, or other litigation-specific information that supports efficient review. We also provide support for document management.

Comprehensive Quality Control

Quality assurance remains an essential component of every paper discovery project. Before documents are finalized, our specialists perform multiple quality control checks to verify image quality, page completeness, indexing accuracy, OCR performance, coding consistency, and production specifications. This detailed review minimizes errors while ensuring documents meet client requirements and court expectations.

Secure Storage & Preservation

Many matters require continued access to original paper files long after scanning has been completed. Cornerstone Discovery offers secure storage solutions designed to protect sensitive records while maintaining organization and accessibility throughout ongoing litigation.

Production Preparation

The final phase involves preparing documents for production according to opposing counsel, court orders, or client specifications. Whether producing native images, searchable PDFs, TIFF files, or other requested formats, we guarantee litigation copying is accurate, complete, and delivered on schedule.
