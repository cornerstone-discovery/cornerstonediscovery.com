Discovery Management for Large, Complex, Multi-Defendant Criminal Cases

Federal and multi-defendant cases arrive with more discovery than any one defense team can open: wiretap recordings and line sheets, jail calls, pole camera and surveillance video, phone extractions, financial records, and productions in formats nobody has a player for. And it keeps coming, in rolling productions, for months.

Managing that discovery is what we do. Cornerstone Discovery has supported criminal defense teams in multi-defendant mega-cases in state and federal courts for years. We take in every production, make all of it readable and searchable, and get it to every defense team on the case, from the first disclosure through trial.

Every defendant gets the whole case, not just the parts someone had time to open.

Start With a Free Data Assessment

Before you commit to anything, send us the discovery, or tell us what the government has produced so far. We inventory it at no cost: how much there is, what kinds of files it holds, which recordings and videos need special players or conversion, what can’t be searched yet, and what it will take to make all of it usable. You get a clear plan and estimate, which is also what you need in hand when you request funding for the case.

With or Without a Coordinating Discovery Attorney

When the court appoints a Coordinating Discovery Attorney (CDA), we work under the CDA’s direction as the team that processes, hosts, and distributes discovery to every defense team in the case.

When there is no CDA, we handle the technical side of that role for the defense: one place that receives the government’s productions, organizes them, tracks what has arrived, and gets them to every attorney who needs them. Either way, defense counsel deal with one team that knows the whole production.

Built for Multi-Defendant Discovery

  • One database for the whole defense. Every defense team works from the same complete, searchable set of discovery, while each team’s own notes, tags, and searches stay with that team.
  • Rolling productions, kept straight. Supplemental productions and superseding indictments are loaded as they arrive, checked against what was already produced, and marked so every team can see what is new.
  • Formats that won’t open. Title III wiretaps and line sheets, jail calls, Cellebrite reports, non-searchable PDFs, unknown file types, and video locked in proprietary players from ATM cameras, pole cameras, and Hawk systems, all converted so they play and search in a browser.
  • Access for the whole team. Attorneys, paralegals, investigators, and experts can review from anywhere, with access set case by case.

Transcription and Translation

Hours of recordings are only useful once someone can read them. We transcribe wiretaps, jail calls, interviews, and other recordings, along with depositions and hearings, and sync transcripts to the audio or video so your team can read along and jump straight to the moment that matters.

When discovery includes foreign-language documents, we translate them, so they can be searched and reviewed alongside the rest of the production.

Junto, Built for Federal Criminal Discovery

Our review platform, Junto, was built in-house to manage large federal criminal discovery. Documents, audio, and video live in one place, searchable from any browser, without the cost and complexity of a platform designed for corporate civil litigation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the free data assessment include?

We review the discovery produced so far, or your description of it, and report what is there: volume, file types, recordings and video that need conversion, and material that can’t be searched yet. We then give you a plan and an estimate for making all of it usable. There is no cost and no obligation.

Do you work with Coordinating Discovery Attorneys?

Yes. When a CDA is appointed, we work under the CDA’s direction to process, host, and distribute discovery to every defense team. When there is no CDA, we can manage the discovery for the defense directly.

Can each defense team keep its work product private?

Yes. Every team reviews the same discovery, but each team’s notes, tags, and searches are visible only to that team.

What kinds of discovery can you handle?

Documents, email, financial records, Title III wiretaps and line sheets, jail calls, surveillance and pole camera video, phone extractions and Cellebrite reports, and files in proprietary or unknown formats. If a file came with its own player, we can usually convert it.

Do you work outside Pennsylvania?

Yes. We manage discovery for defense teams in state and federal courts nationwide. Productions can be delivered to us securely, online or on drives.
