Digital Forensics Expert Witness Services

Our certified digital forensic examiners have been qualified as experts in local, state and federal courts throughout the United States. We examine the evidence, write reports that stand up to cross-examination, rebut opposing experts, and testify at depositions, hearings and trials, for criminal defense attorneys and for civil litigants.

The value of an expert is in the explanation. Our examiners translate technical findings into clear conclusions, and are just as clear about what the evidence does not show.

What We Provide

  • Consulting experts who evaluate the evidence early, before you decide whether to disclose a testifying expert
  • Forensic examinations of phones, computers, cloud accounts and carrier records, under documented chain of custody
  • Expert reports, declarations and affidavits
  • Testimony at depositions, suppression and evidentiary hearings, and trial
  • Rebuttal of opposing experts’ methods, reports and conclusions
  • Litigation help: discovery requests, forensic examination protocols and cross-examination outlines for the other side’s expert
  • Courtroom exhibits that make the findings visual, prepared with our trial support team

Areas of Testimony

  • Cell site analysis and call detail records
  • Mobile device forensics: iPhone and Android extractions
  • Computer forensics: user activity, USB devices, internet history and deleted files
  • Data theft, trade secrets and non-compete disputes
  • Cloud, email and social media accounts
  • Video, DVR and surveillance footage, timestamps and metadata
  • Spoliation and evidence preservation
  • Encryption, passwords and database analysis

Our Experts

Every engagement is led by a named, certified examiner. Their certifications include the IACIS Certified Forensic Computer Examiner (CFCE), AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE), Cellebrite Certified Mobile Examiner (CCME) and Physical Analyzer (CCPA), and Magnet Forensics certifications in computer, mobile, cloud, macOS and video forensics.

How an Engagement Works

  1. Talk it through. Call us with the facts and the evidence you have. We will tell you what a forensic examination can and can’t answer before you commit.
  2. Preserve and examine. We collect or receive the evidence, preserve it with documented chain of custody, and examine it with validated tools and methods.
  3. Report. You get findings you can use: a consulting memo, or a formal expert report written to be disclosed.
  4. Testify. We prepare with you before the hearing or trial, then testify to the findings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have your experts testified in federal court?

Yes. Our examiners have been qualified as experts in local, state and federal courts, in criminal and civil matters.

Do you only work for the defense?

No. We work with criminal defense attorneys, public defenders and court-appointed counsel, and with plaintiffs, defendants, corporations and public entities in civil matters.

Can you take a case outside Pennsylvania?

Yes. We work with attorneys nationwide. Evidence can be collected on site, remotely, or shipped to our lab, and our experts travel to testify.

Can you review another expert’s report?

Yes. Rebuttal is a large part of our work. We check the opposing expert’s methods and conclusions against the underlying data and explain where they hold up and where they don’t.

What do you need from me to get started?

A summary of the case, the evidence or discovery you have, and your deadlines. If devices still need to be preserved, call us first so nothing is lost.
