Solutions for Recovering Deleted Files

When a file is deleted from a computer, the operating system typically removes the reference to it, marking the space it occupied as available for new data. The actual data often remains on the hard drive until it is overwritten. This creates a crucial, time-sensitive window of opportunity for recovery. As a file recovery company, Cornerstone Discovery helps individuals, lawyers/law firms, and businesses recover corrupted, lost, or deleted data. Our digital forensics experts will investigate the data loss incident and recommend ways to salvage and restore the data.

The Digital Forensics Process at Cornerstone Discovery

At Cornerstone Discovery, we see digital forensics as more than just the act of recovering data. Rather, it’s a structured, court-admissible process that uncovers, preserves, analyzes, and presents digital evidence with accuracy and integrity. Our expert forensic investigators follow a meticulous, defensible protocol that ensures digital evidence stands up to scrutiny in litigation, regulatory investigations, and internal audits.

  • Identification of Evidence: The process begins with a detailed consultation to identify potential sources of digital evidence. This may include looking at laptops, smartphones, cloud accounts, network servers, or external storage devices. Our team then works closely with clients to map out the digital environment and prioritize critical data sources while maintaining chain-of-custody from the start.
  • Preservation of Data: We use industry-leading tools and forensic imaging techniques to create bit-for-bit replicas of the original data without altering it. This ensures that all metadata, timestamps, and hidden information remain intact, making the evidence legally admissible and forensically sound.
  • Data Collection: Once preserved, our digital forensic experts collect relevant data from various systems using secure and repeatable methods.
  • Analysis and Reconstruction: Our team analyzes file metadata, user activity, communication logs, and internet history to uncover patterns, detect anomalies, or support specific legal theories. Advanced techniques such as keyword searching, timeline building, and data carving are employed to extract actionable insights.
  • Reporting and Expert Testimony: After the analysis, our digital forensics consulting team compiles a comprehensive report that clearly explains findings in plain language supplemented by visuals, timelines, and supporting documentation. When needed, we can provide sworn testimony to explain methodologies, validate evidence integrity, and defend our conclusions in court.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I need digital forensics services?

You may need digital forensics if you’re involved in litigation, suspect data theft or fraud, experience a data breach, or require recovery of lost or deleted information.

Is digital forensics different from e-Discovery?

While both involve handling electronic data for legal purposes, digital forensics focuses on the recovery and analysis of hidden or deleted data and the investigation of digital activity. Electronic discovery is the broader legal process of collecting, reviewing, and producing electronically stored information (ESI) during litigation. Cornerstone Discovery provides both services to ensure end-to-end support for your legal matters.

What types of devices can you collect data from?

Our team of certified digital forensic examiners works to extract critical data from nearly any electronic device, including:

  • Computers and laptops
  • Smartphones and tablets
  • External hard drives and USB drives
  • Servers and network storage
  • Cloud storage accounts (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.)
  • Email systems and communication platforms
Why should I hire a professional for digital forensics investigations?

Attempting to recover or examine digital data without proper training can result in permanent data loss, altered metadata, or compromised chain of custody, all of which can weaken your case. In contrast, by hiring a digital forensics expert, you can have confidence in the integrity, accuracy, and admissibility of the evidence.

At Cornerstone Discovery, our specialists use industry-approved tools and methodologies to ensure all data is collected and analyzed in a legally defensible manner. We also provide detailed reporting, expert consultation, and courtroom testimony when needed.

Can deleted files be recovered?

Even when data is deleted, remnants may remain on a device until they are overwritten. Our data recovery expertise enables us to recover deleted emails, documents, messages, and logs, often revealing crucial insights in investigations.
