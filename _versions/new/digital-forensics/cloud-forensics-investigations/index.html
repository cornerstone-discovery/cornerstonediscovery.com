What Is Cloud Forensics?

Cloud forensics is the process of identifying, preserving, collecting, and analyzing digital evidence stored in cloud-based environments. Unlike traditional forensic investigations, it focuses on data stored across cloud platforms rather than physical devices.

Cloud forensics builds upon the same proven methodologies used in digital forensics, helping preserve cloud-based evidence while maintaining the chain of custody. This helps ensure digital evidence remains reliable for legal investigations and court proceedings.

When Is Cloud Forensics Needed?

Organizations rely on cloud-based platforms for everyday business operations, making them an increasingly valuable source of digital evidence. A timely forensic investigation can help preserve important information before it changes, while providing attorneys and investigators with reliable evidence to support informed legal decisions.

  • Employee Misconduct: Investigate unauthorized account access, policy violations, or suspicious user activity.
  • Business Disputes: Preserve cloud-based communications and documents that may be relevant to litigation.
  • Trade Secret Theft: Examine cloud accounts for evidence involving confidential information or intellectual property.
  • Internal Investigations: Review user activity and document history during workplace investigations.
  • Regulatory Matters: Collect and preserve cloud-based electronically stored information (ESI) using defensible forensic methods.
  • Data Loss or Deletion: Investigate deleted or modified cloud data to better understand what occurred.

What Can Be Collected During a Cloud Data Investigation?

A cloud data investigation focuses on preserving and analyzing information stored across cloud environments while maintaining the integrity of the evidence. Depending on the platform and the needs of the investigation, our forensic specialists can collect a wide range of digital information.

  • Cloud Email: Business email communications, attachments, folders, and related metadata.
  • Shared Documents: Files, revisions, permissions, and collaboration history.
  • Cloud Storage: Documents, images, spreadsheets, presentations, and synchronized files.
  • User Activity: Login history, account activity, and available audit information.
  • Communication Records: Messages and shared content stored within cloud collaboration platforms.
  • Cloud Metadata: File timestamps, ownership information, and document history that may support an investigation.

Our Cloud Forensics Process

Every investigation is unique, but preserving evidence using consistent forensic procedures is essential. Proper data acquisition during digital forensic investigations helps protect the integrity of cloud-based evidence while providing clear documentation throughout the process.

  • Evidence Identification: Determine the cloud accounts, users, and data sources relevant to the investigation.
  • Evidence Preservation: Secure cloud-based information before it can be altered, deleted, or overwritten.
  • Remote Forensic Collection: Perform remote forensic collection when appropriate to preserve evidence without disrupting normal business operations.

  • Reporting: Prepare detailed reports that clearly explain investigative findings and supporting evidence.
