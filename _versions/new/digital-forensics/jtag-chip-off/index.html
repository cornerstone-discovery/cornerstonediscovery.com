Locked iPhone and Android Forensics

We get into locked phones.* Passcode-protected, encrypted, and disabled iPhone and Android devices, including the latest models and operating systems, and we recover the full file system: every message, photo, app database, location point, and deleted record the phone still holds.

Our lab runs the most advanced mobile forensic tools on the market, from Cellebrite and Magnet Forensics, the same tools used by federal, state, and local law enforcement. Our examiners are certified on them and testify to their results.

A locked phone is not a dead end.

Passcode Recovery and Before First Unlock (BFU) Extractions

Most phones reach our lab locked, powered off, and in Before First Unlock (BFU) state, when they’re most heavily encrypted. We recover passcodes and extract data from phones in both BFU and After First Unlock (AFU) states, so the evidence on a seized phone, or one whose owner can’t or won’t unlock it, is still in reach.

Full File System (FFS) Extractions

A full file system extraction is the most complete copy of a modern phone there is, far beyond the logical and backup extractions most labs produce. It includes the keychain, app data from WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Snapchat, and more, system logs, location and usage history, health and activity data, and deleted records that never show up in a standard extraction. If the other side’s report was built from a partial extraction, an FFS often tells a different story.

  • Current iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, and other Android devices on current operating systems.
  • Locked, encrypted, disabled, and BFU devices.
  • Full file system extractions, decoded into reports attorneys can read, with the raw data preserved and hash-verified for court.
  • Independent review of the government’s extraction: what it missed, and whether its conclusions hold up.

Damaged Devices: JTAG and Chip-Off

When a phone has been submerged, burned, or crushed, we go straight to the hardware. JTAG reads the memory through the device’s test access ports, and chip-off removes the memory chip and images it directly. Chip-off is destructive to the device, so we use it when nothing else will work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get into a locked iPhone or Android phone?

Yes. We recover passcodes and extract data from locked, encrypted, and disabled iPhone and Android phones, including current models and operating systems, using Cellebrite and Magnet Forensics tools. Results depend on the exact make, model, and operating system version.

What is a Before First Unlock (BFU) extraction?

A phone that has been restarted or powered off and not unlocked since is in Before First Unlock (BFU) state, when most of its data is encrypted. A BFU extraction recovers data from a phone in that state. Most seized phones arrive this way, and we handle them routinely.

What is a full file system (FFS) extraction?

A full file system extraction is the most complete copy of a modern phone available. It includes the keychain, app data, system logs, location history, and deleted records that logical and backup extractions leave out.

Can you review the government’s phone extraction?

Yes. We check the prosecution’s extraction and report against the raw data, find what was missed or misread, and explain it in a report, in preparation for cross-examination, or on the stand.

What if the phone is physically damaged?

Water, fire, and crush damage don’t always mean the data is gone. JTAG and chip-off let us read the memory straight from the hardware when the phone won’t power on.

Do you work on cases outside Pennsylvania?

Yes. We work with attorneys nationwide. Phones can be shipped to our lab with chain of custody documented, and our experts travel to testify in state and federal courts.
