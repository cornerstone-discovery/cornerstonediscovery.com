Early Case Assessment (ECA) Services

Early case assessment (ECA) is a critical process in litigation that allows legal professionals to quickly evaluate the scope, risks, and potential costs of a case. By leveraging advanced technology and forensic expertise at the outset, attorneys gain a preliminary view into pertinent data, empowering them to make informed decisions long before the most expensive phases of discovery begin.

This proactive approach mitigates risk and provides the insight needed to build a winning strategy.

The Key Advantages of Early Case Assessment

By investing in an in-depth look at the data related to your criminal or civil litigation case, you unlock significant advantages that position your team for success.

Substantial Cost Reduction and Control

Traditional eDiscovery models often involve collecting, processing, and hosting massive volumes of electronically stored information (ESI), much of which is ultimately irrelevant to the case. Since the costs associated with reviewing all this data can be astronomical, many attorneys choose to invest in early case assessment, which reduces the data volume before it enters the most expensive review phase.

By applying sophisticated culling techniques (such as date range filtering, keyword analysis, file type exclusion, and de-duplication), we can eliminate a significant percentage of non-responsive data. This reduction translates directly into lower processing fees, smaller hosting requirements, and, most importantly, thousands of saved hours in manual document review.

Proactive Risk Mitigation

In addition to saving you money, ECA helps you identify and understand potential risks hidden within your data. For example, our analysis can quickly uncover “smoking gun” documents, communications that indicate liability, or evidence that weakens your position. Discovering this information early allows your team to develop a strategy to address it proactively rather than being caught off guard later in the proceedings.

Our process can also identify sensitive information, such as personally identifiable information, protected health information, and trade secrets, to make sure this data is handled appropriately and avoid compliance issues or inadvertent disclosure.

Informed and Agile Case Strategy Development

By understanding the key players, the timeline of events, and the core communications early on in discovery, you can make more strategic decisions. For example, you will have the data to support answers to such questions as, “Should you pursue an aggressive litigation path? Or is an early settlement more advantageous?” This early insight allows your team to focus its resources on the most promising legal theories and avoid wasting time and effort on arguments that are not supported by the available evidence.

How We Approach eDiscovery Early Case Assessment

As part of the ECA process, we pair our digital forensic expertise with advanced software to provide a granular view of your case data.

Based on a discussion with your legal team, our litigation support experts will identify all potential sources of ESI, including email servers, laptops, mobile devices, cloud storage accounts, and collaboration platforms. Once the relevant data sources are identified, our forensic experts ensure that all potentially relevant information is preserved in a legally defensible manner and entered into our powerful eDiscovery tool.

During the next phase, we reduce the volume of data using proven culling and filtering techniques. Finally, we translate our findings into a clear, actionable report, and our eDiscovery consultants walk you through the findings and provide recommendations on how to proceed.
