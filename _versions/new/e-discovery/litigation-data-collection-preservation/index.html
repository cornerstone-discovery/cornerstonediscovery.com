Meeting Legal Preservation Obligations

Federal and state courts continue to place significant emphasis on proper eDiscovery preservation procedures, identifying, preserving, and collecting relevant digital evidence in a defensible manner. This may include:

  • Emails and attachments
  • Cloud-based files and collaboration platforms
  • Mobile device data
  • Network drives and shared folders
  • Text messages and chat applications
  • Databases and enterprise systems
  • Backup media and archived information

Proper forensic data collection ensures legal teams can demonstrate that reasonable and defensible preservation measures were implemented from the start.

Reducing the Risk of Spoliation

Spoliation occurs when evidence is lost, altered, deleted, or destroyed. In today’s digital environment, this can happen quickly if preservation efforts are not implemented immediately. In fact, automatic deletion policies, employee device changes, cloud synchronization updates, and routine data management practices can all unintentionally compromise important evidence.

The good news is that Cornerstone Discovery helps organizations minimize spoliation risks by implementing structured legal data preservation strategies tailored to each matter. Our team works closely with counsel and IT personnel to identify relevant data sources, suspend routine deletion processes when necessary, and preserve evidence in a forensically sound manner.

Maintaining Data Integrity and Admissibility

Courts and opposing counsel may scrutinize how digital evidence was collected and preserved. If evidence cannot be authenticated or if collection methods appear unreliable, its admissibility may be challenged. 

To maintain chain of custody and preserve metadata, our team utilizes proven methodologies and advanced technologies to preserve the integrity of electronically stored information throughout the collection process. In this way, we help ensure that evidence remains accurate, complete, and defensible in legal proceedings.
