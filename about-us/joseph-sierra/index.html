Joseph Sierra

Director, Digital Forensic Services · ACE, MCFE, MCCE, MCME, MCVE

Joseph Sierra leads Cornerstone Discovery’s digital forensics practice and is a nationally recognized expert in cell site analysis and location evidence. Before joining Cornerstone in 2020, he spent ten years in a national wireless carrier’s law enforcement relations department, where he reviewed more than 6,000 cellular reports and testified in more than 400 trials involving mobile data, GPS tracking, electronic surveillance and timing advance evidence.

He has been qualified as an expert witness in local, state and federal courts throughout the United States in cell site and cellular record analysis, radio frequency principles, GPS and cellular triangulation, and cellular forensics.

Areas of Expertise

  • Cell site analysis and historical call detail records from every major U.S. carrier
  • Cellular network architecture, radio frequency principles and timing advance data
  • Mobile device forensics, including full file system extractions of iOS and Android devices
  • Cloud and social media account forensics
  • DVR collection and video forensics
  • Expert reports, rebuttal of opposing experts, and testimony

Experience

At the carrier, Joe began as a legal compliance specialist responding to subpoenas and court orders, and rose to Senior Testifier, leading a team of seven expert testifiers and writing the policies that standardized their work. He led the department’s training and outreach, teaching cellular networks, call detail record analysis and location evidence to prosecutors’ offices and law enforcement agencies, including the FBI.

At Cornerstone, he directs forensic operations and examinations, and works with defense and civil counsel on cases that turn on mobile and location evidence. He has presented CLE programs on cellular records for criminal defense bar associations and public defender organizations.

Certifications and Training

  • AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE)
  • Magnet Forensics Certified Forensic Examiner (MCFE), Cloud Examiner (MCCE), macOS Examiner (MCME) and Video Examiner (MCVE)
  • Hawk Analytics CellHawk training and cellular technology, mapping and analysis courses
  • GrayKey and VeraKey mobile extraction training
  • Carrier network training in GSM, UMTS, LTE and VoLTE architecture and operations

Education

  • M.S., Information Technology Auditing and Cyber Security, Temple University
  • M.S., Criminology, Intelligence and Forensic Data Analysis, Saint Joseph’s University, magna cum laude
  • B.A., Criminal Justice, Rutgers University
