Benjamen McCollum

Senior Digital Forensic Examiner · CFCE, ACE, CCME

Ben McCollum has been a digital forensic examiner at Cornerstone Discovery since 2016, helping attorneys in civil and criminal matters nationwide collect, preserve and examine digital evidence. His work covers computers, mobile devices and cloud services, along with call detail records and historical cell site location information.

He has provided expert testimony in state and federal courts, and works with counsel to explain technical findings in clear terms, including the limitations that affect how the evidence can be read.

Areas of Expertise

  • Mobile device forensics, as a Cellebrite Certified Mobile Examiner
  • Computer forensics: Windows and macOS systems, file systems and registry analysis
  • Call detail record and cell site analysis, including help drafting carrier subpoenas
  • Cloud account collection and analysis
  • Corporate investigations and data theft

Experience

Before joining Cornerstone, Ben served as a Homicide Technical Services Specialist with the Philadelphia District Attorney’s Office, supporting investigations through digital forensic analysis and technical trial support. His background also includes secure data migration, cloud systems and technical support. He co-wrote an article on cell phone technology and cell tower triangulation for the Pennsylvania Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers.

Certifications and Training

  • IACIS Certified Forensic Computer Examiner (CFCE); member, International Association of Computer Investigative Specialists
  • AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE)
  • Cellebrite Certified Mobile Examiner (CCME), Certified Physical Analyzer (CCPA) and Certified Logical Operator (CCLO)
  • Hawk Analytics cellular technology, mapping and analysis, and CellHawk training
  • Drone forensics training

Education

  • B.S., Digital Forensics, Bloomsburg University of Pennsylvania (a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense Education)
