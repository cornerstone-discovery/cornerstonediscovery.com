Benjamen McCollum

Senior Digital Forensic Examiner · CFCE, ACE, CCME

Benjamen “Ben” McCollum is a Senior Digital Forensic Examiner at Cornerstone Discovery, where he has worked since January 2016. He assists attorneys in civil and criminal matters nationwide through the collection, preservation, and examination of digital evidence. His work includes examining computers, mobile devices, and cloud services, as well as analyzing call detail records and historical cell site location information.

Ben has provided expert testimony in state and federal courts. He works with counsel to explain technical findings in clear terms, with attention to what the evidence supports and the limitations that may affect its interpretation.

Areas of Expertise

  • Mobile device forensics, as a Cellebrite Certified Mobile Examiner
  • Computer forensics: Windows and macOS systems, file systems, and registry analysis
  • Call detail record and cell site analysis, including help drafting carrier subpoenas
  • Cloud account collection and analysis
  • Corporate investigations and data theft

Experience

Before joining Cornerstone, Ben served as a Homicide Technical Services Specialist with the Philadelphia District Attorney’s Office, supporting homicide investigations through digital forensic analysis and technical trial support. His background also includes work in secure data migration, cloud systems, and technical support.

Certifications and Training

  • IACIS Certified Forensic Computer Examiner (CFCE); member, International Association of Computer Investigative Specialists
  • AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE)
  • Cellebrite Certified Mobile Examiner (CCME), Certified Physical Analyzer (CCPA), and Certified Logical Operator (CCLO)
  • Hawk Analytics cellular technology, mapping, and analysis, and CellHawk training
  • Drone forensics training

Education

  • B.S., Digital Forensics, Bloomsburg University of Pennsylvania (a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense Education)
