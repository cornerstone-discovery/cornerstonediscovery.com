Kyle McArdle

Senior Digital Forensic Examiner / Director, Information Technology · MCFE, MCCE, MCME, MCVE, ACE

Kyle McArdle is a certified and admitted digital forensic examiner who handles digital forensics and incident response (DFIR) in civil and criminal matters nationwide, and has testified as an expert in state and federal courts. He is also Cornerstone Discovery’s Director of Information Technology. He joined the firm in 2012 as a software developer, and brings a developer’s understanding of how apps and operating systems actually store data to every examination.

Areas of Expertise

  • Mobile device forensics, including iOS full file system and Android analysis
  • macOS, Windows, ChromeOS and Linux examinations
  • Cloud and social media account forensics
  • Video and DVR collection and analysis
  • Encrypted devices, password recovery and deleted data
  • Smart home and connected device data
  • Incident response, volatile memory and malware analysis
  • Reverse engineering, SQLite and raw file structure analysis
  • Deepfake detection and authentication of photos, video and audio
  • AI-generated image analysis and source attribution
  • AI-assisted triage and review of large data sets

Experience

Kyle led development of Junto, Cornerstone’s premier e-Discovery platform, built from the ground up to make document review and production fast, secure and simple for law firms and corporate legal teams. He works mostly in JavaScript and SQL. As Director of Information Technology he runs and secures the firm’s networks, servers and storage, its Windows, macOS and Linux environments, and its cloud infrastructure in Microsoft Azure, including virtual machines, Kubernetes clusters and auto-scaling GPU compute.

Kyle writes and speaks regularly on digital forensics, mobile evidence, AI and emerging technology, from articles for legal publications to continuing legal education programs for attorneys, bar associations and defense organizations.

Artificial Intelligence

Kyle stays at the forefront of AI research and coding in digital forensics. As AI changes both how evidence is created and how it is examined, he works on both sides of the problem. He sits on an industry advisory board that helps shape how artificial intelligence is built into forensic software, and he puts that knowledge to work on real cases.

When a photo, video or voice recording may be a deepfake, Kyle determines whether it is authentic. He examines metadata, compression and encoding artifacts, content credentials and other provenance signals to establish whether media was captured by a real device or generated by AI, and, where possible, which tool or model produced it. That gives attorneys a clear, defensible answer when the authenticity of digital media is in question.

On the investigative side, Kyle builds and applies AI tooling that helps examiners triage massive data sets, surface key evidence faster and spend their time on analysis instead of routine review. Every AI-assisted finding is checked by an examiner, so results stay verifiable and defensible in court.

Certifications and Training

  • AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE)
  • Magnet AXIOM courses in advanced computer, mobile, iOS file system, macOS, cloud and incident response examinations
  • Belkasoft training in Android forensics and AI in digital forensic investigations
