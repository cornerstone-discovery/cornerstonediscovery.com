Kyle McArdle

Certified & Admitted Digital Forensic Examiner · MCFE, MCCE, MCME, MCVE, ACE

Kyle McArdle is a certified digital forensic examiner who collects, preserves and examines electronically stored information in civil and criminal matters nationwide, and has testified as an expert in state and federal courts. He joined Cornerstone Discovery in 2012 as a software developer and moved into forensics, bringing a developer’s understanding of how apps and operating systems actually store data.

Areas of Expertise

  • Mobile device forensics, including iOS full file system and Android analysis
  • macOS, Windows, ChromeOS and Linux examinations
  • Cloud and social media account forensics
  • Video and DVR collection and analysis
  • Encrypted devices, password recovery and deleted data
  • Smart home and connected device data

Experience

Before forensics, Kyle led the development of Cornerstone’s litigation software, including the Junto e-Discovery platform and the Docfol.io exhibit management system, and earlier built training simulations for the U.S. Navy at Lockheed Martin. He is a member of the Magnet Forensics AI Product Advisory Council, an industry group that advises on artificial intelligence in digital forensic tools.

He co-wrote an article on cell phone technology and cell tower triangulation for the Pennsylvania Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers, and has presented CLE programs on mobile device extractions and the admissibility of phone evidence for criminal defense organizations.

Certifications and Training

  • Magnet Forensics Certified Forensic Examiner (MCFE), Cloud Examiner (MCCE), macOS Examiner (MCME) and Video Examiner (MCVE)
  • AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE)
  • Magnet AXIOM courses in advanced computer, mobile, iOS file system, macOS, cloud and incident response examinations
  • Belkasoft training in Android forensics and AI in digital forensic investigations

Education

  • B.S., Digital Media, Drexel University
