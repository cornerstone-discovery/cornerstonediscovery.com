Jason Silva

Managing Director · Certified & Admitted Digital Forensic Examiner · ACE

Jason Silva is the Managing Director of Cornerstone Discovery and a certified digital forensic examiner who has been admitted as an expert in state and federal courts. Since joining the firm in 2010, he has examined hundreds of computers, servers, phones and other devices in civil and criminal matters nationwide, and testified about them in depositions, hearings and trials.

He also led the development of Junto, Cornerstone’s e-Discovery review platform, and has administered databases of hundreds of millions of records, which gives him unusual depth on the e-Discovery side of a case as well as the forensic side.

Areas of Expertise

  • Computer and mobile device forensics in criminal and civil matters
  • Data exfiltration, trade secret and departing-employee investigations
  • Location data, IP addresses and network evidence
  • Video metadata, encryption and database analysis
  • Forensic examination protocols and e-Discovery strategy
  • Expert reports, rebuttal and testimony

Teaching and Publications

Jason has presented CLE programs on digital evidence, location data and complex discovery for federal defender offices and panel attorneys, criminal defense and white-collar bar programs, county bar associations and the Pennsylvania Bar Institute. He has lectured at Widener Law School, Drexel University and Wilmington University, and his writing has appeared in Legal IT Professionals and on The Tech Gazette. He serves on a data, technology and research subcommittee of an indigent defense advisory committee.

Certifications and Training

  • AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE)
  • ACEDS eDiscovery Executive (eDEx) and eDiscovery Advanced (eDAdv) certificates; member, Association of Certified E-Discovery Specialists
  • Autopsy certification (Basis Technology)
  • GrayKey mobile extraction training

Education

  • B.S., Computer Science, Drexel University College of Engineering
