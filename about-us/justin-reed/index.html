Justin Reed

Certified & Admitted Digital Forensic Examiner · CCO, CCPA, ACE

Justin Reed collects, preserves and examines phones, computers and other storage devices for civil and criminal cases, and has been qualified as an expert witness in state courts. He joined Cornerstone Discovery in 2021 on the trial support team, working as an in-court consultant in federal and state courts, before moving into forensic examinations full time.

That courtroom background shows in his work: he knows how a finding will be presented to a jury before he writes it up.

Areas of Expertise

  • Mobile device extractions and analysis
  • Call detail record plotting and analysis
  • DVR and video evidence
  • Windows forensics and deleted data recovery
  • Trial exhibits and in-court presentation of digital evidence

Certifications and Training

  • Cellebrite Certified Operator (CCO) and Certified Physical Analyzer (CCPA)
  • AccessData Certified Examiner (ACE)
  • CellHawk Certified User (Hawk Analytics), with carrier-specific record training
  • Autopsy certification (Basis Technology) and Belkasoft Windows forensics

Education

  • B.S., Digital Forensics, minor in Computer Science, Bloomsburg University of Pennsylvania
